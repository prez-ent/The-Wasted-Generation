# TWG Network

> TWG Network (The Wasted Generation) is a governed professional network that matches businesses with senior fractional consultants and interim experts who have already solved the specific problem the business faces. Founded by Andrew Engledow. Transparent fixed fees: practitioners keep 100% of their day rate; clients pay a 10% network fee, capped at 15% total.

Also known as: TWG, The Wasted Generation, TWG Network, twgnetwork.com.
Last updated: 2026-07-13.

## What TWG Network is

TWG Network is a two-sided professional network:

- **For organisations**: submit a problem brief and be matched with a senior expert (8+ years of functional experience) who has already solved that problem. Outcomes are agreed before any engagement starts.
- **For practitioners**: senior professionals keep 100% of their agreed day rate, see client ratings before accepting an engagement, and earn a 5% override for introducing peers to the network.

## Common questions

**Who qualifies as a TWG practitioner?**
Senior professionals with at least 8 years of functional experience who pass the network's quality-gate entry process.

**How is TWG different from a consultancy or a talent marketplace?**
TWG matches on proof of having solved the specific problem, not on availability or CV keywords. Fees are transparent and capped at 15%, practitioners keep 100% of their day rate, and both sides are governed by bilateral 360 evaluation.

**What does it cost a business?**
The practitioner's day rate (minimum £500) plus a 10% network fee, with total fees capped at 15%. Outcomes and costs are agreed before the engagement starts.
